Research

Our research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence, with particular interests in multimodal learning, federated learning, Bayesian machine learning, uncertainty quantification, and reliable AI for real-world applications.

Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence

Our overarching goal is to build AI systems that are reliable, robust, uncertainty-aware, and privacy-conscious, particularly under imperfect data, distribution shifts, missing information, and complex real-world conditions.

Trustworthy Multimodal Learning

We study reliable multimodal learning from heterogeneous sources such as text, audio, images, video, and structured data, with particular emphasis on missing, noisy, conflicting, and dynamically varying modalities.

Trustworthy Federated Learning

We develop federated and distributed learning methods that address privacy, statistical heterogeneity, personalization, uncertainty, and communication constraints across decentralized environments.

Bayesian and Uncertainty-Aware Learning

We investigate Bayesian deep learning, probabilistic inference, uncertainty quantification, and calibration to help AI systems recognize when predictions are reliable and when additional caution is needed.

Trustworthy AI for Healthcare and Embodied Intelligence

We explore trustworthy multimodal AI for healthcare and embodied intelligence, including multimodal perception, uncertainty-aware decision-making, human-centered interaction, and intelligent systems operating in safety-critical real-world environments.

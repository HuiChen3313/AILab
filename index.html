Advancing Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence

We develop trustworthy AI through multimodal learning, federated learning, Bayesian machine learning, and uncertainty-aware intelligence for real-world applications.

About

Our lab focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence, aiming to build reliable, robust, uncertainty-aware, and privacy-conscious AI systems for complex real-world environments.

Research Areas

Trustworthy Multimodal Learning

Reliable and robust learning from heterogeneous, noisy, missing, and conflicting multimodal data.

Trustworthy Federated Learning

Privacy-aware, uncertainty-aware, and personalized learning across decentralized and heterogeneous environments.

Bayesian and Uncertainty-Aware AI

Principled uncertainty quantification, calibration, probabilistic inference, and reliable decision-making.
